1. About Pitwall

Pitwall is a fan-made Formula 1 companion application providing race schedules, circuit information, driver and team information, live race data, telemetry-related information, and related features.

Pitwall is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Formula 1, Formula One World Championship Limited, or any Formula 1 team, driver, or other rights holder.

2. Information We Collect

Pitwall does not have a developer-managed user account system. The app works without creating an account or signing in.

Pitwall does not request location permissions and does not use GPS or device-location APIs. The “GPS Circuit Radar” feature refers only to a circuit-map visualization and does not access the device’s physical location.

Pitwall does not use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, or advertising services.

CrashGuard crash reports are written to a local file on the user’s device only and are not transmitted to Pitwall servers.

3. Optional Google Play Email Linking

Pitwall may optionally allow a user to link a Google Play-related email address on the device for subscription-restore purposes.

  • The feature is completely optional.
  • The app works fully without linking an email address.
  • The email address is stored locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences (app-local storage).
  • The email address is never sent to Pitwall servers.
  • Pitwall does not use the email address for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

Locally stored app data can be removed at any time by clearing Pitwall’s app data in Android system settings or uninstalling the app.

4. Purchases and Google Play Billing

Pitwall offers optional digital purchases through Google Play Billing, including subscriptions and one-time purchases. Google handles payment processing and billing-related services under Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Pitwall does not receive or store payment card numbers, bank account numbers, or other payment credentials.

5. Local Storage

Pitwall may store app preferences and applicable subscription-related information locally on your device. Locally stored information remains on the device until you remove it, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app.

6. Data Sharing

Pitwall does not sell, rent, or share users’ personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

The optional linked email address is not transmitted to Pitwall servers. Google Play may process information necessary to provide Google Play services, including billing and purchase management, under Google’s privacy practices.

7. Security

Pitwall is designed to minimize collection of personal information. Information stored locally is handled through Android app-local storage mechanisms. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Pitwall does not maintain a developer-side user account database containing the information described in this policy.

Locally stored optional information and CrashGuard files remain on the device until removed by the user, application data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

9. Children’s Privacy

Pitwall is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Pitwall.

10. Third-Party Services

Pitwall may use Google Play services for app distribution and billing. These services are operated by Google under Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Pitwall does not integrate Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, advertising SDKs, or analytics/advertising services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy when Pitwall’s features, data practices, or applicable legal or platform requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.
